HOW WE WORK

Custom Order Process & Lead Times

Everything you need to know about commissioning a bespoke cake for your Sydney event.

STEP 1

Inquiry & Inspiration

Submit your celebration date, guest count, and design references via our quote form or direct WhatsApp. We confirm date availability within 24 hours.

STEP 2

Design Concept & Quote

We discuss tier proportions, color matching, floral elements, and flavor selections. A formal written quote is provided with sketch consultation.

STEP 3

Handcrafting & Handover

Your cake is baked fresh days before your event. Pick up securely boxed at our North Ryde studio or arrange temperature-controlled courier delivery.

ESSENTIAL TIMELINES

Lead Times & Delivery Across Sydney

To guarantee the highest level of craftsmanship, we take a limited number of commissions each week.

Wedding Cakes

Notice: 6 to 12 weeks recommended (minimum 4 weeks).
Handover: On-site delivery and floral placement across Greater Sydney, Hunter Valley, and Southern Highlands.

Milestone Celebrations

Notice: 2 to 3 weeks in advance.
Handover: Studio collection at North Ryde or specialized point-to-point courier delivery.

Rush Orders

Subject to kitchen calendar capacity. Contact us directly on WhatsApp to check last-minute openings for the current week.
